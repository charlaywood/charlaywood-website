What I would like to hear about

  • 🔬 Research collaborations

    Plant specialised metabolism, enzyme discovery and metabolic engineering. Particularly glycosylation, pathway flux, and getting engineered traits out of tobacco and into field crops.

  • 🫘 Commercialisation and industry

    Natural colourants, plant-made ingredients, scale-up for Natural Red Pigments, and anyone who has taken a plant-derived ingredient through regulatory approval.

    Conferences, seminars, workshops, podcasts and press. Topics, past talks and a speaker kit are ready to go.
