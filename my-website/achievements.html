July 2022 · York, UK · Competition Final
Three Minute Thesis: "Alkaloids: For Killing, Curing and Cooking"
The Three Minute Thesis (3MT) challenge asks PhD researchers to explain their work to a non-specialist audience in just three minutes with a single static slide. I was a finalist in the University of York's 2022 competition, presenting on how understanding plant alkaloid biosynthesis could make valuable drugs like morphine cheaper and less wasteful to produce. A brilliant exercise in distilling years of research into something anyone can connect with.
